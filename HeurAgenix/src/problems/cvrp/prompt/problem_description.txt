This module represents a configurable Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP) family.

The active variant is specified by the problem state's constraint profile:
- CVRP: capacitated closed routes without time windows.
- OVRP: capacitated open routes without time windows.
- OVRPTW: capacitated open routes with hard time windows.
- VRPTW: capacitated closed routes with hard time windows.

All V1 variants minimize total travel distance. Capacity remains active. Every route starts at the depot, every customer must be visited exactly once, and open-route variants do not include the final return-to-depot edge in the objective. If time windows are active, service must begin within each customer's [earliest, latest] window; early arrival is allowed and causes waiting, while late service is infeasible.
